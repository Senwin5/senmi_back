About Senmi

Senmi Mobility is a technology-enabled mobility platform operated by Real Time Delivery App Ltd. We are building convenient and reliable ways for people to move around and send packages through ride-hailing and package delivery services powered by technology.

Who We Are

Senmi Mobility is the trading name and technology-enabled mobility brand of Real Time Delivery App Ltd. Our platform is designed to connect customers with convenient transportation and package delivery services.

Through the Senmi mobile application and other approved platform channels, customers can access available services for getting to their destinations and sending packages. Senmi focuses on providing a convenient digital experience supported by technology, customer support, service management, and transparent processes.

Our platform supports different service needs, including ride-hailing for people who need transportation and package delivery for individuals and businesses. Drivers and riders may be required to complete applicable registration and verification processes before accessing the relevant services.

We are committed to making everyday movement easier through reliable services, technology, safety, accountability, and responsive customer support.

How Senmi Mobility Operates

Senmi Mobility uses technology to help customers access transportation and package delivery services. Our platform supports service requests, coordination, updates, and customer support for the services available to them.

Convenient Ride-Hailing

Customers can use available ride-hailing services through the Senmi platform to request transportation to their destinations.

Reliable Package Delivery

Individuals and businesses can use available package delivery services to arrange the movement of packages from pickup to their destinations.

Verified Drivers and Riders

Applicable registration and verification processes help support accountability and service standards for drivers and riders using the platform.

Our Driver and Rider Network

Senmi Mobility works to build a dependable network of drivers and riders to support its available mobility services. Depending on the service and applicable arrangements, drivers and riders may operate as independent contractors using the Senmi platform.

Drivers and riders may be required to complete registration, identity checks, document submission, and other applicable verification processes before becoming eligible to receive service requests.

Drivers and riders are expected to fulfil accepted service requests professionally, treat customers respectfully, follow applicable traffic and transportation laws, and comply with the relevant Senmi operational and safety requirements.

Where applicable, compensation for completed services is handled in accordance with the relevant driver or rider agreement and payout policy. Any earnings or balance displayed in a driver or rider application represents the applicable internal earnings record and is subject to the platform's relevant terms and payout processes.
