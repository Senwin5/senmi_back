Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about deliveries, payments, riders, and using the Senmi Logistics platform.

Customers create delivery requests through the Senmi Logistics app by entering pickup and delivery locations, package details, and recipient information. Once payment is confirmed, an eligible rider is assigned to complete the delivery. Customers can track their package until it is successfully delivered and verified.

Delivery requests are made available to eligible riders based on availability and operational requirements. Approved riders may accept requests and complete the delivery on behalf of Senmi Logistics.

Deliveries are confirmed using a secure delivery verification code provided to the sender or receiver. This helps ensure packages are delivered to the correct recipient.

After a delivery has been successfully completed and verified, Senmi Logistics calculates the contractor compensation payable to the rider in accordance with the company's contractor payout policy.

The rider wallet is an internal dashboard that records contractor earnings for completed deliveries. It does not represent customer funds held on behalf of riders.

Eligible riders may request payment of their available contractor earnings. Approved payouts are made by Senmi Logistics to the rider's registered bank account in accordance with the company's contractor payout schedule.

Yes. Riders complete our onboarding and verification process before they are eligible to fulfil delivery requests on the platform.

New rider applications are reviewed by our team before activation. This helps maintain the safety and quality of our delivery services.

You can contact Senmi Logistics through our Contact page, WhatsApp support, or email. Our support team is available to assist with delivery, payment, and account enquiries.
