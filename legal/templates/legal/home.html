How Your Delivery Works

From creating your delivery request to confirming delivery, Senmi makes sending packages across Lagos simple and reliable.

1
Create a Delivery Request

Enter the pickup location, delivery location, package details, and receiver information.

2
Get Your Delivery Cost

Senmi calculates the estimated delivery fee based on the distance.

3
Pay Securely

Pay for the delivery yourself or generate a payment link for the receiver.

4
Get Matched With a Rider

After payment is confirmed, a nearby rider is assigned to the delivery.

5
Track Your Package

Follow the delivery from pickup to the destination with delivery updates.

6
Confirm Delivery

The receiver confirms delivery using a secure delivery code.
