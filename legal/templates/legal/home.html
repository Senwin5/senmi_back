TRANSPARENT OPERATIONS

How Package Payments Work

Package customers purchase delivery services from Senmi. Approved independent riders engaged by Senmi complete delivery services.

Customer Purchases Delivery

Customers pay for package delivery services using the payment options supported by Senmi Mobility.

Delivery Is Completed

An approved independent rider engaged by Senmi Mobility completes the delivery service.

Contractor Compensation

Contractor compensation is calculated according to Senmi's applicable contractor payout policy.

Contractor Payout

Approved contractor payouts are made according to Senmi's payout policy and schedule.

Independent Rider Model

Customers purchase package delivery services directly from Senmi, the package delivery brand operated by Real Time Delivery App Ltd.

Riders using the Senmi platform operate as independent contractors engaged by Senmi Mobility to fulfil delivery services accepted through the platform.

Rider compensation represents payment by Senmi Mobility for completed delivery services, according to the applicable contractor terms.

The rider wallet is an internal earnings dashboard used to display unpaid contractor compensation for completed deliveries. It is not a customer payment wallet and does not hold customer funds on behalf of riders.
