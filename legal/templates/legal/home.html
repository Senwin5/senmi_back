CLEAR & TRANSPARENT

How Payments and Rider Earnings Work

Senmi Logistics provides a secure and transparent payment process for customers and a structured earnings process for riders.

1. Customer Pays Securely

The customer or receiver pays the full delivery fee securely through the Senmi Logistics platform. Payment is processed through our authorised payment service provider and received by Senmi Logistics.

2. Commission Is Calculated

Senmi Logistics calculates its service commission from the delivery fee. The commission currently consists of a ₦200 base fee plus 10% of the delivery price.

3. Rider Earnings Are Credited

After Senmi's commission is calculated, the remaining delivery balance is recorded as the rider's earning and credited to the rider's wallet on the platform.

Rider Withdrawal and Settlement

Riders do not receive delivery payments directly from customers. Riders can view their available earnings in their Senmi wallet and request a withdrawal. Approved withdrawals are processed to the rider's registered bank account.

Transparent Payment Flow
Customer payment, commission, rider earnings, and withdrawal records are managed through the Senmi Logistics platform.
Example: If a delivery fee is ₦5,000, Senmi's commission is ₦700 (₦200 plus 10% of ₦5,000). The remaining ₦4,300 is recorded as the rider's earning.
