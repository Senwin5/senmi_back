What to include

Please send your request from the email address associated with your Senmi account and include your registered phone number so that we can verify your account.

Customer accounts

For customer accounts, account deletion is permanent.

Once a customer's deletion request has been verified and processed, the customer's account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted, except for information that Senmi is required or permitted to retain for legitimate purposes such as legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

Rider accounts

Rider accounts follow a separate deletion process because riders participate directly in Senmi's delivery operations.

When a rider requests account deletion, the rider account is first placed into deactivated status. The rider will no longer be able to access or use Senmi rider services.

The deactivation period allows Senmi to review the rider's account for any outstanding deliveries, transactions, disputes, payments, security matters, or other unresolved issues.

Rider deletion review
1
Account deactivated
Rider access to Senmi services is disabled.
2
Account reviewed
Senmi Support or authorized administrators review the account for unresolved matters.
3
Permanent deletion
If there are no outstanding matters requiring retention, the rider account may be permanently deleted.
Rider records that may be retained

Where necessary, Senmi may retain certain records while an outstanding matter is being resolved or where retention is required or permitted for legitimate purposes.

  • Delivery and order records
  • Payment and transaction records
  • Rider earnings and payout records
  • Delivery disputes and related records
  • Security and fraud-prevention records
  • Records required for legal, regulatory, or accounting purposes
Permanent deletion after review

When there are no outstanding matters requiring the rider's information, the rider account can proceed to permanent deletion in accordance with Senmi's data-retention practices.

Data removal

Senmi removes personal information that is no longer required for legitimate business, legal, security, financial, or operational purposes.

Information that must be retained for legitimate purposes is handled in accordance with Senmi's Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.

For more information about how Senmi collects, uses, protects, and retains information, please see our Privacy Policy .
