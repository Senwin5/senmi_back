Delete Your Senmi Account

You can request the deletion of your Senmi account and associated personal data by contacting Senmi Support.

How to request account deletion

If you no longer want to use your Senmi account, you can request account deletion by contacting our support team.

Account Deletion Request
senmisupport@gmail.com

Please send your request from the email address associated with your Senmi account. Include your registered phone number so that we can verify and identify your account.

What happens when you request deletion?

When a deletion request is processed, your Senmi account is placed into deactivated status. You will no longer be able to use the account normally or access Senmi services through that account.

Deactivation is used to protect the security and integrity of Senmi's delivery operations and to ensure that important records relating to deliveries, transactions, disputes, payments, security, and other operational activities are not improperly removed.

How your data is handled

Some personal information associated with a deactivated account may remain in Senmi's systems where it is necessary for legitimate operational, security, legal, fraud-prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or regulatory purposes.

Information that is no longer required for these purposes may be removed or anonymized in accordance with Senmi's data-retention practices.

Data that may be removed

  • Account information that is no longer required
  • Profile information that is no longer required
  • Personal information that is no longer needed for legitimate business or legal purposes
  • Other information that can be safely removed without affecting operational, legal, or security requirements

Data that may be retained

Senmi may retain certain records after account deactivation where reasonably necessary for:

  • Delivery and order records
  • Payment and transaction records
  • Rider earnings and payout records
  • Customer and rider disputes
  • Fraud prevention and security investigations
  • Legal and regulatory compliance
  • Accounting and financial record keeping

Retained information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and is handled in accordance with Senmi's Privacy Policy.

Important

Deactivating your Senmi account does not necessarily mean that every record associated with the account is immediately and permanently removed. Certain records may need to be retained for security, delivery accountability, legal, financial, or other legitimate purposes.

Need help?

If you have questions about your account, personal information, or the deletion process, please contact Senmi Support.
