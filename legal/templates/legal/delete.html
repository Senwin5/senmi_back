Delete Your Senmi Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can request the deletion of your Senmi account and associated personal data using the information below.

How to request account deletion

If you want to delete your Senmi account, please contact our support team using the email address below.

Deletion Request Email
senmisupport@gmail.com

Please send your request from the email address associated with your Senmi account and include your registered phone number so that we can identify your account.

What happens when your account is deleted?

When your account deletion request is approved, Senmi will delete your account and the personal information associated with it, subject to any information that we are required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or regulatory purposes.

Data that may be deleted

  • Your account information
  • Your name and contact information
  • Your account profile information
  • Information associated with your Senmi account where applicable

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or financial/accounting records.

Need help?

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal data, contact Senmi Support.
