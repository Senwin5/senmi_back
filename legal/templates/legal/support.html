Senmi Support Center

Real Time Delivery App Ltd

Welcome to the official Senmi Support Center. We are committed to providing reliable support for passengers, customers, approved ride drivers, delivery riders, and business users on the Senmi Mobility platform.

Senmi Mobility supports ride-hailing and package delivery services. Whether you need help with booking a ride, a trip, package delivery, driver or rider verification, payments, compensation and payouts, tracking, technical issues, or your account, our support team is available to assist you.

1. Passenger & Ride-Hailing Customer Support

Passengers can contact Senmi support for assistance with ride-hailing services, including:

  • Ride booking and driver assignment issues
  • Difficulty finding or contacting an assigned driver
  • Trip delays and cancellations
  • Incorrect pickup or destination details
  • Fare and trip charge disputes
  • Failed or duplicate payment transactions
  • Ride history and trip status concerns
  • Lost property during a trip
  • Driver conduct complaints
  • Account login and verification issues
  • Ride tracking and notification problems

When reporting a ride-related issue, please provide the trip reference or other available booking details, the approximate trip date and time, and a clear description of the problem. Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, or sensitive payment credentials.

2. Package Delivery Customer Support

Customers can contact Senmi support for assistance with package delivery services, including:

  • Package tracking issues
  • Delayed or unsuccessful deliveries
  • Customer payment verification
  • Failed or duplicate payment transactions
  • Refund requests and payment-related concerns
  • Incorrect pickup or delivery information
  • Missing, damaged, or misdelivered packages
  • Delivery disputes
  • Delivery confirmation problems
  • Account login and notification issues

When contacting support about a package, provide the available delivery reference, relevant dates, and a description of the issue. Our team will review the information and advise on the appropriate next steps.

3. Ride Driver & Delivery Rider Support

Approved ride drivers and delivery riders can contact Senmi support for assistance with their accounts and service activities. Support may cover:

  • Driver or rider profile submission and approval
  • Identity, licence, vehicle, and document verification
  • Profile correction and verification review updates
  • Ride or delivery assignment concerns
  • Ride earnings, delivery compensation, and account records
  • Payout processing and payment discrepancies
  • Bank account verification and payout concerns
  • Account restrictions and access problems
  • GPS, location, and live tracking issues
  • Application crashes and technical difficulties
  • Safety concerns and reports of inappropriate conduct

Drivers and riders should keep their profile, contact, vehicle, and payment information accurate and up to date. Profile approval and access to ride or delivery services may depend on the applicable verification requirements and platform procedures.

Earnings and payout records are subject to verification and the applicable payment and contractor procedures. Contact support if you believe a record is incorrect or a payout has been delayed.

4. Customer Payments, Fares, Compensation & Payouts

Senmi support can help investigate payment and account-record concerns relating to ride-hailing and package delivery services. These may include:

  • Failed customer payment transactions
  • Duplicate charges or disputed transactions
  • Ride fare or delivery charge discrepancies
  • Payment confirmation problems
  • Refund requests, where applicable
  • Driver or rider earnings discrepancies
  • Driver or rider payout delays
  • Bank account verification issues
  • Suspected fraudulent transactions

Payment reviews, fare adjustments, refunds, and driver or rider payouts are handled in accordance with the applicable service terms, transaction records, and platform procedures. Contact support with the relevant trip or delivery reference and transaction details so the issue can be reviewed.

Important Security Notice: Senmi support will never ask you to disclose your account password, OTP, ATM PIN, card PIN, or other sensitive banking credentials. Do not share these details with anyone claiming to represent Senmi.

5. Technical Support

If you experience technical problems while using Senmi Mobility, our support team can help investigate issues involving:

  • Login or authentication errors
  • Application crashes, freezing, or loading problems
  • Ride booking and driver assignment issues
  • Package booking and tracking problems
  • GPS, location permission, or map errors
  • Live trip or delivery status updates
  • Notifications not being received
  • Profile, document, or image upload failures
  • Payment status not updating correctly
  • Slow application performance
  • Network synchronization problems

To help us investigate technical issues, please provide your device type, a clear description of the problem, and screenshots where appropriate. Do not include passwords, OTPs, or sensitive financial information in screenshots.

6. Safety & Security

The safety and security of passengers, customers, drivers, and delivery riders are important to Senmi Mobility. Contact support if you need to report:

  • Suspicious account activity or attempted fraud
  • Unauthorized payment activity
  • Harassment, threats, or abusive behaviour
  • Suspected driver or rider impersonation
  • Unsafe driving or unsafe delivery conditions
  • Concerns about a driver, rider, passenger, or customer
  • Lost property during a ride
  • Stolen, missing, or damaged packages
  • Misuse of ride or delivery confirmation codes
  • Unauthorized access to an account

Reports may be reviewed and investigated. Depending on the circumstances, violations of platform policies may lead to account restrictions, suspension, termination, or referral to the appropriate authorities where necessary.

Emergency Notice: If you are in immediate danger or there is an urgent medical or security emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency services first. Contact Senmi support when it is safe to do so. Support channels should not be treated as a substitute for emergency services.

7. Response Times

Senmi aims to review support requests as quickly as reasonably possible. Response and resolution times may vary depending on the nature of the issue, the information provided, and whether further investigation is required.

  • Urgent safety concerns — prioritized for prompt review
  • Active trip or delivery issues — prioritized where possible
  • Customer payment concerns — reviewed as soon as reasonably possible
  • Driver and rider verification — subject to document review and applicable procedures
  • Compensation and payout concerns — subject to transaction checks and payout procedures
  • Technical investigations — may require additional time
  • General inquiries — reviewed as soon as reasonably possible

Any response estimates are indicative only and do not guarantee that a request will be resolved within a particular period.

Personal information submitted to support may be handled in accordance with the Senmi Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements. Please provide only the information reasonably necessary to investigate your request.
