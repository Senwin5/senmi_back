Senmi Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Senmi Mobility, a technology-enabled mobility platform operated by Real Time Delivery App Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose personal information when you use the Senmi mobile application, website, ride-hailing services, package delivery services, driver and rider services, and related technologies.

Senmi Mobility is designed to support convenient transportation and package delivery services. The features available to you may depend on your location, account type, device permissions, and the services currently offered through the platform. By accessing or using Senmi, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Senmi platform, we may collect information including:

  • Full name and account profile information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Pickup, destination, and delivery addresses
  • Ride requests, trip details, and delivery information
  • Location and GPS information, where permitted
  • Payment, transaction, and refund information
  • Device, application, and technical information
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) notification tokens
  • Driver and rider registration and verification information
  • Driver's licence, vehicle, and other required verification documents
  • Profile photographs and other uploaded images
  • Vehicle details and registration information, where applicable
  • Trip, delivery, payment, and account history
  • Ratings, reviews, and service feedback, where available
  • Customer support communications and reports
  • Information required to administer driver or rider compensation, where applicable

We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service, account function, security purpose, or legal obligation.

2. How We Use Your Information

Senmi may use collected information to:

  • Create, maintain, and manage user accounts
  • Provide and manage ride-hailing services
  • Provide and manage package delivery services
  • Process ride requests and delivery requests
  • Connect customers with eligible drivers or riders
  • Coordinate trips, pickups, destinations, and deliveries
  • Calculate fares, delivery charges, and applicable fees
  • Process payments, refunds, and transaction records
  • Verify driver and rider identities, documents, and eligibility
  • Provide trip and delivery status updates
  • Support navigation, location tracking, and service coordination where enabled
  • Maintain trip, delivery, and service records
  • Calculate and administer driver or rider compensation, where applicable
  • Provide customer, driver, and rider support
  • Send service-related notifications and account updates
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unsafe activity, and unauthorized access
  • Investigate complaints, incidents, and disputes
  • Improve the security, reliability, and performance of our services
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

3. Customer Payments and Services

Customers may use Senmi Mobility to request available ride-hailing and package delivery services. Payments, fares, delivery charges, and other applicable fees are handled according to the relevant service terms and payment arrangements.

Customer payments may be processed through authorized third-party payment service providers. Senmi may retain transaction and payment information necessary to confirm payments, provide customer support, prevent fraud, process refunds where applicable, and maintain business records.

Senmi does not intend to collect or store customers' payment card PINs, payment passwords, or other sensitive banking credentials. Payment information is handled according to the arrangements and security practices of the applicable payment service provider.

Depending on the service and payment method, relevant information such as payment status, transaction reference, amount, and date may be processed or retained to manage the service and resolve payment-related questions.

4. Driver and Rider Information and Compensation

Drivers and riders using the Senmi platform may be required to submit registration details, identity information, photographs, licence information, vehicle details, and other documents necessary for verification and eligibility checks.

Depending on the service and applicable arrangements, drivers and riders may operate as independent contractors or under other relevant service arrangements. Their information may be used to assess eligibility, coordinate service requests, maintain service records, address complaints, and support platform safety.

Where applicable, driver or rider earnings represent compensation associated with completed services and are administered according to the relevant agreement, platform rules, and payout arrangements. Compensation records are used for accounting, payment administration, dispute resolution, and compliance purposes.

Any earnings or balance displayed within the Senmi application represents the relevant internal earnings or transaction record. Its meaning and use depend on the applicable service and payout arrangements. It should not be interpreted as a bank account, deposit account, or escrow account unless expressly established under separate applicable terms.

Where necessary to process approved compensation payments, Senmi may collect and use relevant payout details, including bank account information. Such information is handled for payment administration, verification, fraud prevention, record keeping, and compliance purposes.

5. How We Share Information

We may share relevant information where reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, protect, or support Senmi services. Depending on the service, this may include sharing information with:

  • Customers and assigned drivers or riders for trip or delivery coordination
  • Authorized payment service providers
  • Cloud hosting and technology service providers
  • Notification and communication service providers
  • Identity verification and fraud prevention providers
  • Mapping, navigation, and location service providers, where used
  • Professional advisers and service providers where necessary
  • Government agencies, regulators, courts, or law enforcement where legally required

Information shared between customers and drivers or riders may include relevant names, contact details, pickup or destination information, delivery details, and trip or service status, to the extent necessary to fulfil the relevant request.

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We may use service providers to process information on our behalf for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Location, Trips, and Delivery Information

Senmi may collect or process location and GPS information when permitted by your device settings and when reasonably necessary to provide available ride-hailing, navigation, package delivery, tracking, safety, fraud prevention, or operational features.

Depending on the features you use, location information may be processed to identify pickup points and destinations, coordinate drivers or riders, provide trip or delivery updates, support navigation, and help resolve service issues.

Where location access is enabled, drivers or riders may share relevant location information while using certain service features. Customers may be able to view a driver's, rider's, trip, or delivery location information when needed to coordinate an active request and where that feature is available.

You can generally manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may affect the availability or functionality of features that depend on location information.

7. Notifications

Senmi may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), email, SMS, and other communication technologies to send relevant notifications, depending on the information and communication channels you provide.

Notifications may relate to:

  • Ride requests and trip status updates
  • Driver assignment and arrival updates, where available
  • Package pickup and delivery confirmations
  • Account registration and verification
  • Driver or rider approval and service assignments
  • Payment, refund, and compensation updates
  • Security alerts and account activity
  • Customer support updates
  • Administrative announcements
  • Promotional information where permitted

You may manage certain notifications through your device settings or other available communication preferences. Important service, account, or security communications may still be sent where necessary and permitted.

8. Data Security

Senmi uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service, electronic system, or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting their account passwords and keeping their login information confidential.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing our services, maintaining trip and delivery records, administering payments, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, preventing fraud, and complying with applicable legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, applicable legal requirements, and whether information is needed to resolve an ongoing complaint, investigation, or dispute.

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, anonymize it, or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable requirements, subject to any lawful retention obligations.

10. Account Suspension and Security Reviews

Senmi may review accounts and investigate activity where reasonably necessary to protect users, maintain platform integrity, enforce applicable terms, or meet legal obligations. This may include activity associated with:

  • Fraudulent activity or suspected payment fraud
  • False or misleading account information
  • Fake driver or rider verification documents
  • Unauthorized payment activity
  • Abusive, threatening, or unsafe behavior
  • Illegal or prohibited service activity
  • Repeated violations of platform policies
  • Misuse of the Senmi application or services
  • Security risks or suspected unauthorized access
  • Reports concerning trips, deliveries, drivers, riders, or customers

Depending on the circumstances, action may include verification requests, temporary restrictions, account suspension, account termination, or reporting to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

11. Third-Party Services

Senmi may use third-party services to support payment processing, notifications, cloud hosting, analytics, mapping, navigation, security, communication, and other platform functions.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies, their agreements with Senmi, and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Where third-party services are integrated into the platform, their collection and use of information may also be subject to the terms and privacy notices applicable to those services.

12. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion where applicable, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may also manage certain device permissions, including location access and push notifications, through your device settings. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention, dispute resolution, and record keeping.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided below. We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

13. Children's Privacy

Senmi's services are not intended to be used by children in ways prohibited by applicable law or the terms of the relevant service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so is prohibited.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Senmi in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Senmi may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, operational practices, technology, legal requirements, or regulatory obligations.

When material changes are made, we may provide notice through the Senmi website, mobile application, email, or other appropriate communication channels. The revised policy will state its effective date.
